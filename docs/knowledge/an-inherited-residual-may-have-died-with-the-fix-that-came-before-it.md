A finding routed from one task to another travels as a **sentence**. The measurement that justified
it does not travel with it — and by the time the receiving task is worked, one or more fixes have
landed in between.

## The failure shape

1. Leaf A measures a population and cannot attribute part of it. It routes the residual to a new
   leaf B, honestly, with the evidence it had.
2. Leaf A′ lands a fix for the part it *could* attribute.
3. Leaf B is worked. Its premise is read from the routing note and taken as the starting point,
   because it was measured, recently, by a careful predecessor.

Step 3 is where it goes wrong. The residual was a property of the artifact **at the time of leaf A**.
Here the producing rule had in fact been removed *before* leaf B was even opened — by a sibling fix
nobody connected to the residual, because nothing re-ran the measurement that would have shown the
rows dying.

⇒ **A routed residual is a hypothesis with a timestamp, not a finding with a lifetime.**

## What this does NOT mean

It is not an argument against routing, and it is not "the earlier leaf was sloppy". Routing the
residual was correct; so was requiring the new leaf to be sequenced after the pending fix. Both
disciplines worked — and it was precisely the sequencing rule that forced the re-derivation that
caught it. The gap is narrower and more fixable: **nothing re-ran the measurement between the two
leaves**, so a residual that had already died stayed on the books as live work.

## The practice

1. **The first act of an inherited leaf is to re-derive its premise at HEAD** — with the original
   instrument if it is tracked, and against the artifact as it is now. Budget it as part of the work,
   not as a formality.
2. **Carry a density control.** A re-derivation that finds nothing has to prove the sample could have
   found something: here, 0 self-rejects over 7 994 rows only means anything alongside "128 of those
   rows carry a value-position arrow". A zero over a sample with none of the shape is vacuous
   ([[a-control-that-cannot-fail-is-not-a-control]]).
3. **Attribute the death, do not just note it.** "It no longer reproduces" is a status; "`H.16.6b`
   replaced the key rule that generated it, so the class cannot recur" is a root cause, and only the
   second tells you whether to close the leaf or keep watching.
4. **Re-check the routing note's other clauses too.** A premise that is stale in one clause is worth
   reading adversarially in all of them — the same note also miscounted the admissible routes.
5. If nothing re-runs the measurement between leaves, that missing watch is itself the finding —
   route *it*, with the cost you just paid as its evidence.
