⚠️ THE ONE NON-OBVIOUS RISK, recorded BEFORE running the gates rather than
   discovered by a red one: a census-inert change is not automatically a
   CERTIFICATE-inert change. These 12 tokens are REGEX terminals, and the stimuli
   generator synthesizes witnesses FROM the regex, so a widened body can change
   the GENERATED witness text (e.g. a `1\t'b\t0` sample where the old body could
   only yield `1'b0`) — visible to the witness column and to closed-loop replay
   even though no rule was added or removed. That risk is why both cert gates and
   `sv_stimuli_quality_gate` were run rather than argued away.
   MEASURED OUTCOME: the union gate's witness column did NOT move (1346, as
   pinned), so the widened alternation did not disturb witness classification.
