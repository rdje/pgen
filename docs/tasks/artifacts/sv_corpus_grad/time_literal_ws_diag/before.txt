GOVERNING LAW — IEEE 1800-2017 Annex A (A.8.4 + footnote 44), verbatim:
  time_literal44 ::= unsigned_number time_unit | fixed_point_number time_unit
  time_unit      ::= s | ms | us | ns | ps | fs
  44) "The unsigned number or fixed-point number in time_literal shall
       not be followed by a white_space."
  unsigned_number33    ::= decimal_digit { _ | decimal_digit }
  fixed_point_number33 ::= unsigned_number . unsigned_number
  33) "Embedded spaces are illegal."

  => TWO independent constraints, both violated by :
     (A) no white space at the number<->unit seam           [footnote 44]
     (B) the number is unsigned_number OR fixed_point_number
         -- NOT the full  (no based literals, no exponent reals) [A.8.4]
