GRAMMAR-WELLFORMED.H.16.6f — THE TWO CLAIMS I PUBLISHED ABOUT THE INSTRUMENT, RE-CHECKED
=========================================================================================

`-0183` published that `--directed-generation-goal duality_break` is *"strictly stronger"* than the
own-corpus sampling `H.16.6c`–`H.16.6e` used, and (in layer A) *"~100× cheaper"*. The director
challenged the findings callout. Both halves were re-derived BY MEASUREMENT rather than by
re-reading, because a re-read repeats its own blind spot.

CLAIM 1 — "it scores against the SHIPPED parser, not the interpreter"          ✅ VERIFIED
  Not from the CLI docstring (which is the author's own description) but from the refusal the
  binary itself carries:
    rust/src/main.rs:3094
      "goal duality_break needs --features generated_parsers
       (the real generated parser is the rejection oracle)"
    rust/src/main.rs:3120
      "goal duality_break: grammar '{}' has no registered generated parser to hunt against"
  ⇒ the oracle is the shipped artifact. This half stands.

CLAIM 2 — "strictly stronger"                                                  ⛔ REFUTED as stated
  The adversarial steering does NOT always beat undirected sampling. From this leaf's OWN census —
  the evidence was already on the page when the claim was written:
      regex   directed  9/1000   diverse baseline 11/1000   <- the BASELINE out-found the search
      ebnf    directed 176/1000  diverse baseline 141/1000   <- here the search wins
  ⇒ "stronger" is true of the ORACLE, not of the SEARCH. A wired gate must read BOTH arms; reading
  the directed arm alone would have under-reported regex by 2 rows.

CLAIM 3 — "~100× cheaper"                                                      ⛔ REFUTED: ~3.4×
  Equal sample count, same grammar (`semantic_annotation`), 1 000 samples:
      duality_break                              4.41 s   (generation AND scoring)
      score_corpus.py over a 999-row own corpus 14.83 s   (scoring ALONE; generation extra)
  ⇒ ~3.4×, and the honest reading is generous to the instrument because its 4.41 s includes the
  generation the other number excludes. The multiplier was REASONED, NOT MEASURED, and it was wrong
  in the direction that flattered the finding.
  ⭐ This repo has recorded this exact failure before: `CI-PARITY-GATE-ROT.32`'s "honest bound" was
  also reasoned rather than measured and also false in the flattering-to-my-caution direction —
  which is why re-reading can never catch it and a command must.
