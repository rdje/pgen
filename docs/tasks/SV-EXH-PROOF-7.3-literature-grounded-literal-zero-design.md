## 0. TL;DR (the tangible proof the director asked for)

**Literal-0 is systematically attainable. It is NOT a structural limit, and the
field has solved this exact problem for decades.** Our 888-residual plateau and our
three failed "steering" experiments are a *textbook* failure mode (mode collapse /
over-exploitation), and the literature's prescription is unambiguous:

> **Decouple the two jobs.** Keep a *diverse background corpus* generated by an
> **unbiased** sampler; then satisfy each of the residual uncovered obligations
> with **its own deterministic minimal witness** (Purdom shortest-derivation),
> **appended** to the corpus — never by reshaping the shared generator's
> distribution. One minimal witness per uncovered production ⇒ production-coverage
> residual = **literal 0**, and because witnesses are only *added*, background
> diversity is untouched.

This is Purdom's 1972 result (a finite set of short sentences exercising every
production), confirmed empirically by Hennessy & Power (2005/2008) — full grammar
rule coverage on real grammars (ISO C++) is reached *only by constructively adding
the last witnesses, not by sampling more* — and matches our own prior root-cause
note that the residual is "a REACHABILITY problem, not a weighting one"
([[project_sv7_never_selected_rootcause]]). Our existing `forced_or_branch_for_site`
/ reach-path machinery is exactly the PEG-forcing layer the plan needs.

## 2. The field's name for our problem, and the coverage-criteria taxonomy

Our task — "generate stimuli that exercise every reachable production/branch of an
EBNF/PEG grammar" — is **grammar-based test generation** under a **grammar
coverage** criterion. "888 uncovered" is only meaningful *relative to a criterion*:

| Criterion | Definition | Origin | "Literal-0" meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Rule / production coverage (RC)** | every production used ≥ once in some derivation | Purdom 1972 | finite, well-defined, **provably attainable** |
| **Context-dependent rule/branch coverage (CDRC/CDBC)** | every production used in *each distinct context* (occurrence) | Lämmel, FASE 2001 | larger target set; well-defined |
| **k-path coverage** | every k-node path through the grammar graph (k=1 ≡ RC, k=2 ≡ production-in-parent-context …) | Havrikov & Zeller, ASE 2019 | depth/k-bounded; well-defined per k |
| **Controllable combinatorial coverage (CCC)** | bounded combinations of production choices, per-construct interaction strength | Lämmel & Schulte, TestCom 2006 | "literal-0 up to depth d" |

**Action item for `.7.4.1`:** confirm which criterion our closed-loop
`replay_target_count` actually measures (it looks like RC / branch-level RC). This
sets whether literal-0 is bounded-finite (RC) or depth-relative (k-path/CCC).

## 3. Why our steering approach failed — explained by the literature (not by guesswork)

Our record: three steering-intensification attempts each **regressed** via the same
signature — generation_successes *up*, net resolved coverage *down*
(`.7.2.10` quantifier-forcing → 1982; `.7.2.12` → 1717; `.7.2.20` rule-reach →
1883; best non-steered = **888**). The field names this exactly:

- **Mode collapse / over-exploitation.** *RLCheck* (Reddy, Lemieux, Padhye, Sen,
  ICSE 2020) shows a validity/target-hit reward **without a novelty term** makes a
  generator "learn one reliable template and emit it forever." Their fix is a
  **novelty-relative reward** (credit *distinct* coverage, zero-credit repeats) —
  not stronger steering. This is our diversity collapse, precisely.
- **Never reshape the shared distribution; phase-separate exploration vs
  exploitation.** *AFLGo / Directed Greybox Fuzzing* (Böhme, Pham, Nguyen,
  Roychoudhury, CCS 2017) reaches targets by adding *energy* to seeds on a
  simulated-annealing schedule — it never degrades the rest of the corpus. Seed-
  scheduling work (NDSS 2021; USENIX Sec 2024) frames the same exploration/
  exploitation trade-off.
- **Weights give no coverage guarantee.** *DGL* (Maurer, IEEE Software 1990), the
  ancestor of "weight the grammar to steer output": raising a weight to chase a
  rare rule starves the rest. The cautionary precedent — and a description of our
  current generator.

**Conclusion (fact-backed):** steering the *single shared generator* toward targets
is the documented anti-pattern. The lever was wrong, exactly as our `.7.2.20`
negative result concluded — and now we know *why*, with citations.

## 4. The convergent answer — a decoupled 3-layer architecture

All three independent surveys converge on the same shape:

### Layer 1 — Diverse background corpus via an *unbiased* sampler
- **Count-uniform (recursive method):** Flajolet, Zimmermann, Van Cutsem, *TCS*
  1994 — count derivations of size n per symbol, sample proportional to counts →
  provably uniform over derivation trees, curing the shallow-derivation skew that
  starves deep/rare productions (the same skew *Gramatron*, Srivastava & Payer,
  ISSTA 2021, attacks via grammar-automaton normalization).
- **Boltzmann samplers:** Duchon, Flajolet, Louchard, Schaeffer, *CPC* 2004 —
  near-uniform, size-targeted, linear expected time; scales to large
  SystemVerilog-sized inputs.
- **Optional anti-frequency bias:** *Skyfire* (Wang et al., S&P 2017) deliberately
  up-weights *low-probability* rules and caps per-rule reuse — diversity by
  anti-frequency, not by single-target steering.

### Layer 2 — Per-residual **minimal witness** (the literal-0 engine)
- **Purdom's algorithm** (BIT 1972): phase 1 computes a *shortest-derivation table*
  (min terminal length per symbol); generation greedily expands via a not-yet-used
  production, then completes with the shortest derivation. Guarantees **every
  production used ≥ once** with a *small set of short* sentences (ANSI C: 11
  sentences/213 rules; GNU C++: 81/824, per Malloy & Power's reconstruction, ICIS
  2001). Deterministic ⇒ **cannot** suffer diversity collapse.
- **Set-cover framing:** Hennessy & Power (ASE 2005; *EMSE* 2008) treat RC as a
  set-cover obligation and — crucially for us — find that **100% rule coverage on
  ISO C++ is reached only by *adding* Purdom-style witnesses for the last uncovered
  rules, not by collecting more real tests.** Direct empirical evidence that
  "generate more / steer harder" cannot close the tail, but constructive completion
  can.
- **Directly-implementable form:** *The Fuzzing Book* "Grammar Coverage" chapter's
  `GrammarCoverageFuzzer` — a **two-tier** rule at each expansion site: *if any
  reachable child unlocks an uncovered production, take the shallowest such child
  (Burkhardt 1967 shortest-path / BFS); otherwise expand uniformly at random.*
  Global covered-set persists across the corpus. This is the elegant, low-risk
  antidote: bias is **local to the one decision that needs it**, never global.
- **Batch efficiency:** Havrikov & Zeller (ASE 2019) k-path "on the way"
  bookkeeping (their Line 21) = the greedy set-cover heuristic: one minimal witness
  discharges many uncovered targets at once, so the witness *set* stays small and
  structurally diverse by construction.

### Layer 3 — PEG-forcing (we already have this)
Purdom/LR theory assumes CFG reachability; our grammar is **PEG** (ordered choice +
`&`/`!` predicates + the structural gates in [[project_sv7_never_selected_rootcause]]:
depth-floor pruning, recursion-pressure penalty). A production can be
*derivation-reachable* yet *PEG-unreachable*. The shortest derivation says *what to
build*; our existing **`forced_or_branch_for_site`** / `compute_reach_path` /
`reach_classification` machinery is *how to force the PEG to take it*. The "reach
guard" defines the genuinely-**reachable** target set — making "literal-0
*reachable*" a well-defined, bounded goal rather than an unbounded one.

### Optional bridge (unifies layers 1+2 in one sampler)
**Multidimensional Boltzmann sampling** (Bodini & Ponty, AofA 2010): uniform random
generation *while constraining each production to a targeted frequency*. Raise the
target frequency of the residual productions inside an otherwise-uniform sampler →
diverse corpus that hits the stragglers at a guaranteed rate, no single-target
steering. A principled future option if the simple decoupled approach leaves a tail.

## 5. The literal-0 argument (proof of possibility)

1. By **Purdom (1972)** there exists a finite set of short sentences exercising
   every production of a CFG at least once (rule coverage = 0 residual).
2. By **Hennessy & Power (2005)** the practical way to *reach* 0 on real grammars is
   to **add** a constructive witness for each still-uncovered production (set-cover
   completion) — empirically validated on ISO C++.
3. Our residual is **100% reachable** (our own `.7.2.17` classification via
   `reach_classification`; [[project_sv7_never_selected_rootcause]]) — no element is
   structurally dead; each has a forcing path.
4. Therefore: compute the **shortest forced derivation per residual element**
   (Purdom phase-1 table + our PEG-forcing), emit it as a **separate appended**
   witness. One witness per obligation ⇒ residual → **literal 0**.
5. Diversity is preserved because step 4 only *adds* to the corpus; it never
   reshapes the diverse background pass (the thing that collapsed in `.7.2.{10,20}`).

**Honest caveats (signoff):**
- "Literal-0" is relative to the criterion (§2). Under RC it is finite & attainable;
  under k-path/CCC it is depth-bounded. `.7.4.1` must pin which one `replay_target_count`
  encodes.
- The reachable target set is defined by the PEG guard; a grammar-reachable but
  PEG-unreachable production is *correctly* excluded (it is not a real obligation).
- Witness **minimality matters**: forcing without shortest-derivation minimization
  is part of *why* our steered samples bloated/collapsed. Purdom phase-1 is the fix.

## 6. Parser-agnostic mapping to `stimuli_generator.rs`

What we already have (reusable): `compute_reach_path`, `collect_rule_reference_sites`
(rule-reference graph), `compute_rule_reach_target`, `deepest_or_on_path`,
`reach_classification`, `forced_or_branch_for_site`, the closed-loop replay-gap JSON.

What the literature says to **add** — all general, benefits every PGEN parser:

1. **Shortest-derivation (min terminal length) table** — Purdom phase 1: per symbol,
   the length of the shortest terminal string + which production achieves it. Pure
   analysis, fixpoint over the grammar, unit-testable, **zero generation change**.
2. **Decoupling discipline** — emit per-target witnesses as a *separate appended
   corpus segment*; the main diverse pass is left exactly as the 888 baseline (which
   we know is byte-stable and diverse). This is the single most important change and
   the direct fix for our failure mode.
3. **Two-tier expansion policy** (Fuzzing Book) for the *witness* pass: at each site,
   prefer the shallowest child that unlocks an uncovered target (via the min-length
   table + reach graph), else uniform-random; global covered-set across the segment;
   mark "on the way" discharges (greedy set-cover).
4. **(Optional, Layer 1 upgrade)** count-uniform / Boltzmann background sampling to
   cure shallow-derivation bias and shrink the residual that even needs witnesses.

Each step lands one-at-a-time, measured against the global `replay_target_count` with
determinism confirmed, per [[feedback_no_codebase_change_without_tool_backed_facts]].

## 7. Recommendation for the `.7.2.21` director decision

The director set **literal-0** as the bar. This research **demonstrates literal-0 is
systematically attainable** via a decades-studied, diversity-preserving, decoupled
architecture — so the recommendation is **NOT** accept-with-evidence at 888.

**Commission `SV-EXH-PROOF.7.4` — "literature-grounded literal-0"**, phased:

| Leaf | Work | Risk | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.7.4.1` | Pin the coverage criterion `replay_target_count` measures (RC vs context vs k-path) + define the reachable target set under the PEG guard. Pure docs. | none | — |
| `.7.4.2` | Implement the Purdom shortest-derivation (min-length) table as pure analysis (no generation change), unit-tested. | none (analysis only) | lib+clippy green; generation byte-identical to 888 |

| `.7.4.4` | If a tail remains: two-tier uncovered-first witness expansion + (optional) count-uniform background. Measure. | medium | measured, one-thing-at-a-time |

Expected outcome: `.7.4.3` alone should move the residual sharply toward 0 (it is the
Hennessy-&-Power completion step); `.7.4.4` closes any remainder. Literal-0 is the
target, reached by *construction*, not by *sampling harder*.
