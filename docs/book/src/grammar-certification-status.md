## What "certified" means here — and what it does not

Certified means **certificate coverage**: the stimuli generator can produce a string that reaches
every rule of the grammar. It is a claim about the grammar's own internal reachability.

⛔ **It is not a claim that the parser is correct for the language.** A grammar can be fully
certified and still accept invalid input or reject valid input; that is the *corpus* axis, tracked
per family in [Roadmap and Live Status](roadmap-and-live-status.md). For SystemVerilog the corpus
axis currently reads **46.3 % adjudicated** with **275** known defects in the bar.

⚠️ **A certification is only as good as its freshness.** A proof is a statement about one specific
generated parser. When codegen moves, the parser moves, and the proof stops describing it — while
still reading green. Every row therefore carries its freshness beside its verdict:

| freshness | meaning |
|---|---|
| `fresh` | the contract pins the digest of the parser that is in the tree |
| `STALE` | the contract pins a *different* parser — the proof no longer describes what ships |
| `UNPINNED` | the contract carries no identity block, so nothing can say which tree it scored |
| `—` | no contract exists |

## How to read it, and what is owed

- **`NO ORACLE`** does not mean "broken" — it means **nothing has ever scored that family's rule
  reachability**, so there is no certification claim to be true or false. Seven of the nine shipped
  grammars are in this state. A family in this row may be perfectly good; the point is that nobody
  knows, and until now nothing said so.
- **`UNVERIFIED`** means a real green result exists but cannot be trusted at HEAD. Both current
  cases are freshness failures, not coverage failures.
- ⭐ **Why this page exists.** The previous answer to "which grammars are certified?" was a
  **doc-asserted roster** — `CHANGES.md` records that one family's membership *"was doc-asserted
  only"*. A roster nobody derives is a claim that rots silently, and it did: SystemVerilog's proof
  is dated a full day before the parser it describes, and every doctrine still reported PASS.
